Opening paragraph: state the engineering or procurement decision this article helps the reader make.

## Decision context

Explain the boundary of the topic, the information required, and the assumptions that must be confirmed.

## What to review

Use clear sections, short paragraphs, lists, and tables where they materially improve technical understanding.

## Project handoff

Close with the drawings, specifications, quantities, inspection requirements, or other inputs needed for a useful review.

## Sources

For network-based writing, use the structured `externalResearch` source register in frontmatter. Link only to relevant
primary or authoritative sources. Do not invent performance data, certifications, customers, or project outcomes.

If an image is needed, use the structured `cover` attribution fields. If its source and right to use cannot be verified,
publish without that image.
